# Chess Formator Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

Chess Formator ("the app") is an offline chess-coaching app. It is designed so that your data stays on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect, store or sell your personal data.** The developer operates no server that receives your games, analysis, training history, player profile or settings. All of this is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Features that touch your device or other services

- **Local profile and training data.** Games, attempts, review state and preferences are saved in local app storage only.
- **Chess engine and coaching.** Position analysis (Stockfish) and coaching run on your device.
- **Microphone (optional).** If you use voice input, audio is recorded and transcribed on your device by a bundled speech model. Audio is not sent to the developer or to any third party.
- **Bluetooth and location permission (optional).** Android requires these permissions to scan for and connect to supported Chessnut electronic chess boards over Bluetooth. The app does not read, store or share your location.
- **Lichess (optional).** If you choose to sign in with or use features that connect to lichess.org, the app communicates directly with Lichess. Your Lichess access token is kept on your device. Data exchanged with Lichess is governed by the Lichess privacy policy (https://lichess.org/privacy). Chess Formator does not receive it.
- **Model downloads (optional).** If you choose to download additional models inside the app, your device fetches those files from the public host named in the app. No personal information is sent by the app for this.
- **Purchases.** Premium is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the developer does not receive your payment details. See the Google Play privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
